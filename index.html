Chapter IV • Sartorial Grace

Formal Couture & Palette

“Dressed in whispered pastels and timeless Philippine craftsmanship—honoring the sanctity of the house of worship and the romance of the garden evening.”

Bridal Color Reservation

Kindly Reserve Pure Ivory & Pure White Exclusively for the Bride

We deeply appreciate our guests embracing the curated six-shade pastel palette below.

Gentlemen

Barong Tagalog

Long-Sleeved Formal

We kindly invite gentlemen to wear formal attire in our soft pastel palette or classic neutral tones:

  • • Barong Tagalog: Traditional or modern Jusi, Piña, or Organza Barong paired with black or dark charcoal slacks.
  • • Long-Sleeved Formal: Tailored button-down long-sleeved dress shirt in our six-shade pastel palette (or soft neutral) with tailored trousers.
  • • Suits & Blazers: Optional lightweight pastel, beige, or charcoal suit paired with a crisp collared shirt.
  • • Footwear: Closed-toe leather dress shoes, loafers, or formal Oxford shoes with dark socks.

Kindly Avoid

  • Pure Ivory, Pure White, or Cream (reserved for the Bride)
  • Denim jeans, cargo pants, shorts, sneakers, or sandals

Ladies

Modern Filipiniana

Sleeved Pastel Gown

We kindly invite ladies to dress in modest, sanctuary-appropriate silhouettes in any of our six pastel hues:

  • • Modern Filipiniana: Traditional Terno, Alampay, or butterfly-sleeve ensemble in soft pastel silk, chiffon, or lace.
  • • Formal Gowns & Dresses: Floor-length evening gowns, flowing maxi dresses, or modest below-the-knee midi dresses with sleeves.
  • • Sanctuary Modesty: All attire must feature covered shoulders and backs (cap, flutter, three-quarter, or long sleeves).
  • • Footwear: Formal heels, dressy block heels (garden-friendly for the reception), or embellished flats.

Kindly Avoid

  • Pure Ivory, Pure White, or Cream (reserved for the Bride)
  • Sleeveless, strapless, off-shoulder, or backless dresses & gowns
  • Mini dresses, high slits, plunging necklines, denim, or flip-flops
