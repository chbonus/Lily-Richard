Bridal Color Reservation

Kindly Reserve Pure Ivory & Pure White Exclusively for the Bride

We deeply appreciate our guests embracing the curated whimsical pastel palette below.

Gentlemen

Barong Tagalog

Long-Sleeved

Formal attire in soft pastels or classic neutral tones:

  • • Barong Tagalog: Jusi, Piña, or Organza Barong with dark slacks.
  • • Long-Sleeved Formal: Tailored pastel or neutral dress shirt with trousers.
  • • Suits & Blazers: Optional pastel, beige, or charcoal suit.
  • • Footwear: Closed-toe leather dress shoes, loafers, or Oxfords.

Kindly Avoid

  • Pure Ivory, White, or Cream (Bride only)
  • Denim jeans, cargo pants, shorts, or sneakers

Ladies

Filipiniana

Long Gown

Modest, church-appropriate silhouettes in pastel hues:

  • • Modern Filipiniana: Terno, Alampay, or butterfly-sleeve ensemble.
  • • Formal Gowns & Dresses: Floor-length gown or below-the-knee midi dress.
  • • Footwear: Formal heels, garden-friendly block heels, or flats.

Kindly Avoid

  • Pure Ivory, White, or Cream (Bride only)
  • Sleeveless, strapless, off-shoulder, or backless gowns
  • Mini dresses, high slits, denim, or flip-flops
